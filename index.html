01 / About

Making sense of
generative intelligence.

I am a PhD researcher at the Technion, advised by Prof. Tomer Michaeli. My research is in computer vision and machine learning, especially generative models, text-to-image systems, and understanding how neural networks encode and process information.

I am interested in identifying what information models actually need, how that information is represented, and how we can make generative systems more interpretable and controllable.
